Chain Services Co. (HALA SCS) and Tahakom. 
 
This document is prepared and published by HALA SCS exclusively for the use by 
Tahakom employees on a need-to-know basis.

1. Executive Summary 
Hala Supply Chain Services (Hala SCS) is pleased to submit this technical proposal in response 
to Tahakom’s RFP No. 0101000435 for Heavy Logistics Management. This proposal outlines our 
capabilities, operational model, and compliance with Tahakom’s requirements. 
 
Our aim is to provide Tahakom with secure, efficient, and nationwide logistics services that 
enable Tahakom to serve its customers better while aligning with Saudi Vision 2030. The 
proposal demonstrates our experience, fleet readiness, advanced technology integration, and 
commitment to local content development. 
 
The sections that follow detail our company profile, value proposition, service delivery 
approach, and compliance with Tahakom’s technical and operational requirements. 
 
2. Company Profile & Experience 
Hala SCS is a licensed and experienced logistics provider with over 20 years of extensive 
experience, operating across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. We have successfully executed 
projects involving the transportation of heavy-duty, oversized, and sensitive cargo, working with 
both government and private clients.  
Our financial stability, operational structure, and regulatory compliance enable us to deliver 
secure and scalable logistics services nationwide.

4. Scope of Work Delivery Approach 
4.1   Hala SCS will provide end-to-end heavy logistics management services across all 
          Tahakom facilities and designated addresses in KSA.  
4.2   Our operational model covers daily and ad-hoc service requirements supported by 
          a 24/7 command and control center.  
4.3   The process includes secure collection, verification of cargo, loading/unloading, 
          safe transport, and electronic proof of delivery. 
4.4    Pricing will follow per trip/route/load category for predictable cost control.  
4.5    Coverage includes remote and hard-to-reach areas, ensuring service continuity 
           throughout the Kingdom. 
 
5. Security & Risk Management 
5.1      Hala SCS facilities (warehouses and secure yards) comply fully with Tahakom’s 
          requirements for storage of loaded vehicles and materials. 
4.2 All depots are licensed and equipped with 24/7 CCTV monitoring, manned security, pest 
control logs, smoke detection, sprinkler systems, and fire suppression equipment.  
4.3 Emergency signage and alarms are in place throughout.  
4.4 These facilities are integrated into Hala’s Command & Control center for continuous 
monitoring and risk management. 
5.2     All logistics operations follow a robust security framework.  
5.2     Cargo confidentiality is strictly maintained, with drivers screened and cleared for 
          security.  
5.3     Vehicles are GPS-enabled and monitored from our control center.  
5.4     Cargo is sealed with serialized barcoded tags, and secure storage protocols prevent 
          unattended parking.  
5.5     We also follow Civil Defense and Fire Safety compliance.  
5.6     Risk management includes prevention, mitigation, incident response, and alignment 
            with Tahakom’s security policies. 
 
6. Human Resources 
6.1    Hala SCS employs a skilled logistics workforce including drivers, supervisors, and 
        coordinators.  
6.2   We provide an organizational chart and detailed job descriptions for all positions. 
6.3   Our workforce complies with Saudi labor regulations, including GOSI, sponsorship, and 
         medical insurance.  
6.4   Drivers undergo continuous training in safety, SOPs, security, and emergency response.  
6.5   A dedicated single point of contact will liaise with Tahakom on all matters. 
6.6   Each vehicle and facility are equipped with mandatory safety equipment including first  
        aid kits, fire extinguishers, and backup emergency lighting. this ensures full alignment  
         with civil defence and rfp safety requirements.

8. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) 
8.1      Our documented SOPs ensure consistent and reliable operations.  
8.2     These include Billing & Invoicing SOP, Receiving & Verification SOP, Delivery & Pickup 
           SOP, Damage Handling SOP, Quality Control SOP, Load Management SOP, and 
           Emergency SOPs.

10. Sustainability & Vision 2030 Alignment 
10.1    Hala SCS is committed to sustainable logistics.  
10.2    We are implementing a roadmap for clean fuel adoption and carbon footprint 
           reduction.  
10.3    Our initiatives include waste reduction, energy-efficient fleet management, and  
           greenhouse gas offset programs.  
10.4    This directly supports Saudi Vision 2030 objectives in smart mobility and environmental  
           responsibility. 
 
11. Service Level Agreement (SLA) Commitment 
11.1   We commit to Tahakom’s SLA requirements: 
  a.   Express pickup within 2 hours. 
b.   Normal pickup within 24 hours.  
c.   Warehouse pickup window within 4 hours.  
d.   Complaint resolution within 3 days.  
         11.2   Hala SCS will provide an SLA matrix signed and acknowledged to match RFP appendix,  
                  covering express (2h), standard (24h), and complaint resolution (3 days). 
 
 
All SLA compliance will be tracked via real-time systems, with monthly performance reports 
provided to Tahakom. 
 
12. Penalties & Performance Assurance 
12.1     We acknowledge and accept Tahakom’s penalty framework, including SAR 2,500 per 
           day delays, 5% PO liquidated damages, and SLA non-compliance penalties. 
12.2    Our performance monitoring uses KPIs such as on-time delivery %, express service 
           compliance, and cargo integrity.  
12.3    Corrective action protocols ensure immediate resolution of issues to maintain service  
           quality. 
12.4    We acknowledge penalty structure including 0.3% daily deduction up to 10% of PO 
           value, and commit to express delivery at no extra cost if standard pickup is missed. 
 
13. Exit Strategy 
13.1    Hala SCS will submit an exit plan within 3-months of award for Tahakom approval.  
13.2   The plan ensures a smooth transition to Tahakom or a third-party operator without 
          service interruption.     
13.3   It will cover workforce reassignment, data transfer, asset handover, and process 
            continuity.  
Our commitment is to ensure zero disruption during transition.
