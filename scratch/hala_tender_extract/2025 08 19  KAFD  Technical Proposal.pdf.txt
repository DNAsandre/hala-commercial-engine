1. Executive Summary 
Hala Supply Chain Services (Hala SCS) purpose of this project is to facilitate 
accurate stock counting, safe transportation, and secure storage of one restaurant 
tools, furniture, and kitchen equipment currently located at KAFD. This includes 
verifying the existing inventory through a comprehensive physical count, ensuring 
the safe handling and transfer of all items to the storage facility, and providing 
adequate warehouse space for their organized and protected storage. 
Hala’s solution addresses the full scope of KAFD’s requirements for: 
• Stock Count Services 
• Transportation Services 
• Storage Services 
2. Hala SCS Value Proposition 
2.1. Centralized Warehouse Control: 
Hala offers a dedicated or shared, consolidated logistics facility within Riyadh that centralizes all 
KAFD materials — from construction and maintenance stock to tenant deliveries and high -value 
equipment — under one roof. This hub includes integrated office space for KAFD personnel, 
ensuring real-time coordination and operational oversight. 
 
2.2. Strategic Workforce Integration: 
Staff trained in warehouse operations, DG handling, and transport compliance . Escalation 
hierarchy and dedicated office space for up to 6 KAFD representatives ensure streamlined 
coordination. 
 
2.3. Safety, Compliance & Specialized Handling: 
Hala operates licensed, regulation-compliant facilities, equipped to safely store and manage 
dangerous goods, temperature-sensitive chemicals, fragile items, and high-value inventory. 
Our compliance architecture includes fire suppression systems, climate control, material 
classification zones, and safety-trained staff. 
 
2.4. Scalable, Flexible Service Model: 
We offer a scalable model designed for KAFD’s long-term growth, with the ability to expand both 
storage capacity and fleet size in response to project velocity, seasonal peaks, or event -based 
surges. Our operating model supports both dedicated and shared-space options, giving KAFD 
strategic control and financial flexibility.

3. Proposed Solution Description: 
Hala SCS proposes a complete 3PL execution model based on direct alignment with KAFD’s Scope 
of Work: 
• Stock Count Services 
o Conduct a comprehensive physical count and verification of all inventory items 
located at KAFD. 
o Record the description, quantity, and condition of each item.  
o Prepare and submit a detailed inventory report, including photographic evidence of 
all items. 
o Securely wrap and protect all materials prior to transportation from KAFD.  
• Transportation Services 
o Provide appropriately sized trucks with a minimum capacity of ten (10) MT for the 
transfer of materials from KAFD to the Vendor’s designated storage facility.  
o Perform safe loading and unloading operations at both KAFD and the storage facility.  
o Ensure that all transportation activities are conducted in compliance with applicable 
safety regulations and handling procedures. 
• Storage Services 
o Provide a secure, dry warehouse storage area with a minimum size of two hundred 
(200) square meters, to be leased for a mandatory period of three (3) months, with 
the option to renew for additional periods as required. 
o Maintain the storage facility in a clean, organized, and safe condition suitable for the 
nature of the stored materials. 
4. Compliance & Certifications 
• Compliance: The facility is licensed by the Civil Defence, and the municipality has confirmed 
our compliance with the established regulations. 
• Fire & Safety Systems: Full compliance with NFPA standards

• ISO 45001 (Occupational Health and Safety Management Systems 
• Inventory Management System Requirements  
• CCC (Compliance Cyber Security Certificate)  
• SFDA-compliant for regulated inventory 
• Inventory Management: FIFO methodology, real-time tracking.

5. Hala SCS Track Record

Comprehensive Control Co.)

nationwide partnership with Tahakom, supporting their logistics and warehousing 
operations across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Our scope has included the management of 
secure storage, time-critical distribution, and inventory control for advanced technology 
equipment and sensitive infrastructure components.  
 
o Key highlights of our collaboration include: 
 
➢ Nationwide Coverage: Seamless logistics support to Tahakom facilities and sites 
across multiple regions. 
 
➢ Secure Handling: Specialized storage protocols and trained staff for high-security, 
high-value assets. 
 
➢ On-Time Delivery Performance: Consistently maintained an OTIF (On-Time In-Full) 
rate exceeding 98%. 
 
➢ Compliance & Visibility: Full traceability through our WMS system with tailored 
reporting aligned to Tahakom’ s internal KPIs. 
 
This sustained performance reflects Hala SCS’s ability to serve high-profile, security-sensitive 
entities with reliability, precision, and confidentiality. 
 
• SACO:  Track Record with SACO 
Hala has proudly supported SACO, one of Saudi Arabia’s leading retail chains, with

across multiple touchpoints in their supply chain, from centralized distribution to  last-mile 
retail fulfilment.
