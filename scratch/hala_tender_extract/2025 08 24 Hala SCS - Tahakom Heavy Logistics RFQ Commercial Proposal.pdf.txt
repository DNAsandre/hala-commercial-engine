Confidentiality Statement  
 
The information contained within this document is confidential between Hala Supply 
Chain Services Co. (HALA SCS) and Tahakom. 
 
This document is prepared and published by HALA SCS exclusively for the use by 
Tahakom employees on a need-to-know basis. 
  
It contains HALA SCS confidential and proprietary information, and, under no 
circumstances should this document, or a copy, in any form, be given or any of its 
contents disclosed to anyone who is not an authorized recipient.  
 
 
1. Commercial Proposal 
Rates as per submitted template on Ariba portal. 
 
2. Commercial Terms and Conditions 
a. The above rates are exclusive of VAT. 15% VAT will be added to invoices as per local 
regulations. 
 
b. Working 6 days a week, excluding Fridays, Eid Holidays and National Day. 
 
c. Daily Working hours: 10 hours  
 
d. Tahakom shall provide with Hala SCS at least 24 hours in advance notice of any movement 
of Goods, as this will allow Hala SCS to make appropriate arrangements ahead of time. 
 
e. Hala shall be responsible for the following cost  
a. Drivers 
b. Fuel expenses  
c. Cargo Insurance (GIT). 
d. Regular Vehicle Maintenance  
e. GPS 
 
f. Hala SCS is offering rates based on a 3-year contract with option to extend for additional 1 
year term. 
 
g. Tahakom shall pay HALA SCS all due invoices within thirty (30) days of the date of receipt of 
the invoices. 
 
h. The charges are for transportation only, full truck loads. Based on a single drop. 
 
i. Insurance

j. Loading and offloading cargo at Tahakom facilities is the client responsibility. 
 
k. Damages occurred during loading & offloading at customer’s premises are not covered

l. All permits required to enter the loading & offloading sites should be arranged by the 
Customer. 
 
m. The total weight for the cargo must not exceed the tonnage of the specified vehicles, any 
overweight penalty will be under the Customer's responsibility. 
 
n. Truck Cancellation Charge: 
o Any cancellation made 3 hours prior to the scheduled loading time will incur a 
charge of 500 SAR. 
 
o. Detention Charges:  
The time allotted for loading/offloading the cargo is 1 hour. It is essential to adhere 
to these timeframes to ensure smooth operations. Any delay exceeding the allotted 
time for loading or offloading will result in an additional amount of 100 SAR per 
hour as detention charges.    
 
p. Rates are based on January 2025 Fuel Prices. Any increase in the rates of Diesel the quoted 
rate(s) are subject to change based on the following Fuel Surcharge Formula: 
 
For each increase/decrease of Fuel price by 10% the transportation charges will 
change accordingly by 2%. 
 
q. If the government of Saudi Arabia makes any policy changes that reflect on the 
operational cost of transportation business, in such situation, HALA reserves the right to 
increase the rates within the contract period. 
 
r. Tahakom shall ensure that all goods have been properly and sufficiently prepared, packed, 
and labelled in accordance with all applicable laws and regulations and provide HALA SCS 
with such information and assistance as HALA SCS may reasonably request enabling HALA 
SCS to plan and perform the Services, including, avoidance of doubt, information on 
Tahakom productivity and delivery schedules. 
 
s. Mobilization period: 60 Days from the awarding date  
 
t. Tahakom shall ensure that their forecast planning is provided to the Hala Operational 
department a minimum of 2 weeks in advance. 
 
u. Validity of this proposal is 30 days
