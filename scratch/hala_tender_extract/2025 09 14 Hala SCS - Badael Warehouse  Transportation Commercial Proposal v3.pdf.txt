Warehouse Terms and Conditions: 
1. Detailed SLA and KPIs will be agreed between Hala SCS and Badael. 
 
2. Daily Throughput Handling Charges: 
 
2.1 Daily throughout calculated on daily basis. Additional handling charges will apply 
as per above table if daily throughput is exceeded for that day. Daily throughput 
applied each working day Saturday to Thursday. No Daily throughout calculated 
for Fridays/Official Holidays. No monthly or weekly average is considered.  
 
2.2 Increased storage levels do not automatically include additional daily throughput. 
If customer would like to increase or decrease monthly fixed charges/throughput, 
new contract rates need to be agreed by both parties. 
 
2.3 Daily throughput includes up to 10% of Handling Out volumes as Case Picking up 
to a maximum of 14 Pallets per Day. 
 
2.4 Daily throughput includes 0% Inner Pack/Piece Picking, any Inner/Piece picking 
requests will be charged as per the above table. 
 
2.5 Daily throughput will be reported on daily basis by Hala SCS and will be the basis 
for end of month invoice calculation. 
 
2.6 Any pallet received regardless of size will count as 1 Pallet for handling 
throughput. 
 
2.7 Full Pallets dispatched regardless of size will count as 1 Pallet for handling 
throughput, for case picking, each 50 cases “or less” will count as 1 pallet for 
handling throughput. 
 
3. Warehousing Charges are inclusive of Nicotine storage (DG) up to 60 Pallets per month. 
  
4. Warehousing charges are inclusive of office space for up to 7 customer staff. 
 
5. Additional space must be aligned between Hala and Badael 60 days in advance.  
 
6. Monthly storage charges based on highest number of pallets recorded during the month 
as recorded by WMS at the end of each day. 
 
7. Rates are based on agreed pallet dimensions and breakdown below:  
 
a. 30% Euro Pallets L: 1.2m X W: 0.8m X H: 1.6m) 
b. 70% ISO Pallets L: 1.2m X W: 1.0m X H: 1.2m) 
 
8. Mobilization period: 4 Weeks from the awarding date  
 
9. Operating hours: From Saturday to Thursday (1 Shift) from 08:00 AM to 05:00 PM 
(Friday and Public holidays – DAY off)
