Executive Summary 
Hala SCS strongly believes in BADAEL’s vision and mission to provide a tobacco-free 
consumer alternative across the kingdom and soon worldwide which matches Hala’s 
Values and gives Hala the honor to part of this journey as a logistics enabler. 
Hala SCS, being a 3PL major player in the Saudi Market with over 25 years of experience 
across the kingdom focusing on the following industries as core competency: 
• Healthcare. 
• FMCG & Retail 
• Petrochemicals 
• Automotive 
Running warehousing facilities with over 220,000 Pallets Positions capacity in Jubail, 
Dammam, Riyadh, Jeddah as of today with a vision to expand into new geographical 
areas within the kingdom and GCC and expanding the current service offering into End-
to-End transportation including Freight Forwarding, Customs Clearance, Trucking, 
Warehousing, Inventory Management, Distribution, and Bonded Zone.

Hala SCS Value Proposition 
 “HOW CAN HALA SCS HELP BADAEL SERVE ITS CUSTOMERS – BETTER?” 
Badael, being a new company with fast-growing market reach and rapid business 
growth, needs a 3PL partner that has the experience and capabilities to provide the 
required operational agility to Badael as the business dynamics change and Badael 
must rapidly respond to its customers’ demands in existing and new markets. 
 
FMCG Experience 
 
• Over 25 years of experience in warehousing and distribution business. 
• Specialized experience in FMCG & FNB across the kingdom handling major local and 
multi-national brands. 
• Skilled team comes from vari ous FMCG backgrounds. Including FMCG traders, freight 
forwarders, port operators, distributors to add a combination of operational 
understanding for Badael’s needs. 
 
Space Availability & Scalability 
 
• Hala SCS Facility in Jeddah current capacity is 40,000 pallets position with expansion 
plan to add additional 30,000 pallets positions by year 2027.  
• Available space for future seeable or unseeable growth by Badael. 
 
Operational Agility & Flexibility 
 
• Ability to provide a dedicated space for Badael for the needed space with free fully 
equipped office spaces. 
• Agility & Flexibility to change the scope of work as per Badael’s business need to 
split/expand into other geographical area within the kingdom. 
• Hala provides flexibility in case Badael changes its supply chain model from Made to 
Order into Made to Stock model by providing various storage and distribution 
options. 
• Different transportation model service offerings to gives Badael the flexibility to 
achieve more cost-efficient transportation. 
 
Hala SCS Roadmap 2028 
 
Starting in 2025, Hala SCS will expand its service offering to provide End-to-End 
Transportation using digitalization. 
• Customs Clearance by 2026. 
• Container Transportation by 2026. 
• Digital Transportation by 2026. 
• Sea Freight Forwarding by 2027. 
• Last Mile delivery by 2028.
