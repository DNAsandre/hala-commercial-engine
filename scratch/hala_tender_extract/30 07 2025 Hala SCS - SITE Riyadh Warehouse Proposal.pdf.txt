Scope of Work 
a. Dedicated storage space in the Hala Riyadh Tayba warehouse includes two secured and 
fenced areas  of 150 SQM each , a non -fenced storage area (400 SQM) , a fenced and 
secured inspection area (150SQM), and a staging area (150SQM). The total dedicated area 
is 1,000 SQM.  
 
b. Storage Type: TC – 25 Degrees  
 
c. Handling pallets, In and Out activities. 
 
d. Inbound shipments will be inspected for damages and quantity checked against the pre -
alert sent by the client. Any damages or discrepancies will be reported to the client and 
photographic evidence recorded throughout the receiving process. 
 
e. Upon request, any inventory received not palletized, will be palletized and stored 
accordingly.  
 
f. Inbound shipment information will be recorded on the WMS (SKU, Batch Number, Quantity)
