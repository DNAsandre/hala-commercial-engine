# Hala SCS — Company Overview

Hala Supply Chain Services Co. (HALA SCS / HSCS) — founded 1993, KSA. 100% Saudi-owned since 2005 (Basamh Group). 380 FTE, ~211k pallet positions across 9 KSA locations, 240 FTL movements/day, 42-truck temperature-controlled fleet. Local content score ~53–55%.

## Core Verticals

- **Healthcare**
- **FMCG / F&B**
- **Petrochemical**
- **Industrial**

Plus horizontal capabilities: Transport, Warehousing, Forwarding/Customs.

## Systems & Performance

- WMS: EPG LFS
- TMS: Omnialog, integrated with MS D365
- KPI performance: >98% across clients

## Mission / Vision / MTP / Values

- **Mission:** *"Creating Sustainable Value for our Customers."*
- **Vision:** *"To Become the Kingdom's Leading Digital 3PL."*
- **MTP:** *"We Act Differently."*
- **Values:** Extreme Ownership, Excellence Always, Be the Exception, Integrity
- Aligned with Saudi Vision 2030 and Islamic ethics (Amanah, Ihsan, Sidq)
